---
name: direction-audit
description: Zero-based review of whether effort, tests, and documents still serve docs/north-star.md. Run quarterly, or on request when drift is suspected.
---

Read `docs/north-star.md` first, then answer from the repository as it is,
not from its documents' claims: Does effort match the differentiated core?
Are the thinnest-tested surfaces trust boundaries? Do the documents serve
the author or the process? Do they give the human author and the
improvement loop balanced consideration, with tradeoffs between them
recorded rather than implicit? Have the quickstart, the product spec, or
the glossary grown without stated payment? What should be deleted? Record the
findings as a charter under `docs/workbench/` with an execution plan.
Deletions are first-class outcomes, and git history is the archive.
